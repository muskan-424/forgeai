NORDHAVEN SHIP MANAGEMENT - APPROVED SUPPLIER TERMS
Effective 1 March 2025. Applies to all purchases of vessel spares from approved suppliers.

1. Approved suppliers
Four suppliers are approved for vessel spares:
- Hartmann Marine GmbH (Hamburg): engine parts and pumps. Payment terms: net 45 days.
- Pacific Valve Co. (Busan): valves and fittings. Payment terms: net 30 days.
- Baltic Electric AB (Gothenburg): electrical and navigation equipment. Payment terms: net 60 days.
- Orion Safety Ltd (Aberdeen): lifesaving and fire-fighting equipment. Payment terms: net 30 days.
Purchases from any other supplier need prior approval from the technical director.

2. Volume discounts
Hartmann Marine GmbH gives a discount of 3 percent on orders above EUR 5,000, 5 percent above EUR 20,000 and 8 percent above EUR 50,000. Pacific Valve Co. gives 4 percent on orders above EUR 10,000. Baltic Electric AB and Orion Safety Ltd do not give volume discounts. Discounts apply to the order total before freight and are not combined across suppliers.

3. Delivery terms
All deliveries are DAP the port named in the purchase order. Orders marked urgent that the supplier delivers faster than the catalogue lead time carry a surcharge of 15 percent of the order value. Delivery dates are counted in calendar days from the date the purchase order is accepted.

4. Warranty
Standard warranty is 12 months from delivery unless the catalogue states otherwise. Warranty claims must be reported to the supplier within 14 days of the defect being found, with photographs and the part number. Suppliers must replace or repair defective parts within 30 days of an accepted claim.

5. Minimum order values
Hartmann Marine GmbH requires a minimum order value of EUR 500. Orders below the minimum carry a handling fee of EUR 75. The other suppliers have no minimum order value.
