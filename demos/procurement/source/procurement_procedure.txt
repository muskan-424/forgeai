NORDHAVEN SHIP MANAGEMENT - PROCUREMENT PROCEDURE
Effective 1 March 2025. Applies to all purchases for vessels managed by Nordhaven.

1. Approval limits
Purchase orders up to EUR 1,000 may be approved by the vessel superintendent. Orders from EUR 1,001 to EUR 10,000 need approval from the fleet manager. Orders from EUR 10,001 to EUR 50,000 need approval from the technical director. Orders above EUR 50,000 need approval from the board.

2. Quotations
Orders above EUR 2,000 require at least 2 written quotations. Orders above EUR 10,000 require at least 3 written quotations. Quotations must be valid for at least 30 days.

3. Emergency purchases
When a safety-critical item is needed or the vessel risks going off-hire, the superintendent may order without quotations. The purchase must be reported to the fleet manager within 24 hours and approved retrospectively within 48 hours.

4. Classification society items
Parts that affect class or statutory certificates must be supplied with the manufacturer's certificate of conformity. Items without a certificate must be rejected at receipt.

5. Goods receipt
The master or chief engineer must inspect deliveries within 3 working days and sign the goods receipt note. Differences between the delivery and the purchase order must be reported to procurement within 2 working days.

6. New suppliers
A new supplier must pass sanctions screening and a financial check before approval. Suppliers that fail two consecutive deliveries may be removed from the approved list by the technical director.
