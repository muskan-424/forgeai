MERIDIAN MARITIME SERVICES - LEAVE POLICY
Effective 1 January 2025. Applies to shore-based employees. Seafarers are covered by their own employment agreements.

1. Annual leave entitlement
Shore-based employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per leave year (1 January to 31 December). Employees with 5 or more years of service receive 28 days, and employees with 10 or more years of service receive 30 days. The 11 public holidays observed in the Netherlands are in addition to annual leave. Employees who join during the year receive annual leave pro rata to the months remaining.

2. Requesting and carrying over annual leave
Annual leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 14 days before the first day of leave. The line manager must approve or decline within 5 working days. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next leave year and must be taken by 31 March; carried-over days not taken by then are forfeited. Annual leave is not paid out in lieu, except for days accrued but untaken when employment ends.

3. Sick leave
Employees receive full pay for the first 10 days of sick leave in a leave year. From day 11 up to day 60, sick pay is 50 percent of salary. Employees must notify their line manager before 9:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not reduce annual leave entitlement.

4. Parental leave
Birth mothers receive 18 weeks of maternity leave on full pay followed by 21 weeks at the statutory rate. The other parent receives 4 weeks of paternity leave on full pay, to be taken within 6 months of the birth. Employees adopting a child receive the same leave as birth parents. Maternity leave must be notified in writing at least 15 weeks before the expected due date.

5. Compassionate leave
Employees receive 5 days of paid compassionate leave on the death of a spouse, partner, child or parent, and 2 days of paid compassionate leave on the death of a sibling, grandparent or parent-in-law. Compassionate leave is separate from annual leave and sick leave.

6. Unpaid leave
Employees with at least 2 years of service may request unpaid leave of up to 3 months. Unpaid leave requires approval from the department director. Annual leave does not accrue during unpaid leave, and company insurance cover continues only if the employee pays the premium.
