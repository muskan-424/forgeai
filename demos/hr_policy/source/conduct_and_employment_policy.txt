MERIDIAN MARITIME SERVICES - CONDUCT AND EMPLOYMENT POLICY
Effective 1 January 2025. Applies to all shore-based employees.

1. Working hours and overtime
The standard working week is 37.5 hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. Overtime must be approved in advance by the line manager. Approved overtime is paid at 1.25 times the hourly rate for the first 8 hours in a month and 1.5 times after that. Employees may instead take time off in lieu, to be used within 3 months.

2. Remote work
Employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week with the approval of their line manager. Remote work is not available during the first 3 months of the probation period.

3. Probation and notice
New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. During probation either party may end the employment with 1 week of notice. After probation the notice period is 1 month for employees with less than 5 years of service and 2 months for employees with 5 or more years of service.

4. Gifts and conflicts of interest
Employees must not accept gifts or hospitality worth more than EUR 50 from suppliers or customers. Every gift must be recorded in the gift register within 5 working days. Any possible conflict of interest must be declared to the line manager in writing before the employee takes part in the related decision.

5. Disciplinary procedure
The procedure has four stages: a verbal warning, valid for 6 months; a written warning, valid for 12 months; a final written warning, valid for 18 months; and dismissal. Gross misconduct, such as theft, violence or falsifying records, may lead to immediate dismissal without earlier stages. Employees may be accompanied by a colleague or union representative at any disciplinary hearing. An employee may appeal a disciplinary decision in writing within 5 working days.

6. Grievances
An employee who wishes to raise a grievance must do so in writing to their line manager, who must respond within 10 working days. If the employee is not satisfied, the grievance goes to the HR director, who makes a final decision within 20 working days.
