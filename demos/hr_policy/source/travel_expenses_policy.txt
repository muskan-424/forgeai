MERIDIAN MARITIME SERVICES - TRAVEL AND EXPENSES POLICY
Effective 1 January 2025. Applies to all employees travelling on company business.

1. Booking travel
All business travel must be booked through the corporate travel desk at least 7 days before departure. Flights longer than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy with line manager approval; shorter flights are booked in economy. Rail is preferred for journeys under 4 hours. Hotels must be booked through the travel desk and are capped per night by city tier:
- Tier 1 cities (London, New York, Singapore, Tokyo): EUR 180 per night.
- Tier 2 cities (Rotterdam, Hamburg, Dubai, Mumbai): EUR 130 per night.
- All other cities: EUR 100 per night.

2. Daily allowances
Employees travelling within the Netherlands receive a meal allowance of EUR 40 per day. International travel carries a meal allowance of EUR 65 per day. Allowances are paid with the monthly payroll. If meals are provided by the host or included in a conference fee, the allowance is reduced by 25 percent.

3. Claiming expenses
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip, together with original receipts. Claims above EUR 500 require approval from the department head before reimbursement. Approved claims are reimbursed within 14 days. Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected unless the finance director grants an exception in writing.

4. Private car use
Use of a private car for business travel is reimbursed at EUR 0.42 per kilometre. Trips longer than 800 kilometres in one journey need prior approval from the line manager.

5. Travel advances
Employees on trips longer than 5 days may request a travel advance of up to 70 percent of the estimated costs. The advance must be settled within 14 days of return.

6. Costs that are not reimbursed
Alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment and upgrades that were not approved in advance are not reimbursed.
