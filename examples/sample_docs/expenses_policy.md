# Travel and Expenses Policy

## Booking Travel

All business travel must be booked through the corporate travel desk at least 7 days before departure. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy with line manager approval.

## Daily Allowances

Staff travelling domestically receive a meal allowance of 40 euros per day. International travel carries a meal allowance of 65 euros per day, paid with the monthly payroll.

## Claiming Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip together with original receipts. Claims above 500 euros require approval from the department head before reimbursement.
