# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Shore staff are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be requested at
least 14 days in advance through the HR portal.

Unused annual leave may be carried over, up to a maximum of 5 days, into the following year.

## Sick Leave

Employees may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any
absence longer than 3 consecutive days.
